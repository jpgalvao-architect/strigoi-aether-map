# Events and timers

Schedules retain their source timezone. The display timezone in **Settings**
and the interface language do not change when an event occurs. Each row's
tooltip and source button explain its origin.

## Battlefield

The supplied SA Arena of Tactics screen lists daily windows from 11 AM–2 PM
and 7 PM–9 PM. The SA profile uses `America/Sao_Paulo`. While a window is open,
the calendar and floating panel count down to matchmaking closing. Afterward,
they move to the next opening. These are matchmaking windows, not match lengths.

## Immortal Gartua

Enable the timer reader in **Events**. Open Immortal Gartua's details on the
game map, keeping its title and **Time Left** in the upper part of the screen
for a few moments. Two matching readings create a predicted spawn, also
available in the floating panel's event picker.

The reader reuses the image strip already read for menu recognition, without
another continuous capture or saving images. A missing title, a tooltip below
that strip, or failed recognition produces no prediction. Reposition the
map/tooltip and leave the countdown visible to try again.

The prediction stays in memory for this session. Another character, a
disconnection or a different game process clears it. Recheck the countdown
after changing channels. Reaching the predicted time does not prove that the
boss is alive; reopen its details to check the game's current information.

Global community references describe respawns after a kill, but this version
does not incorporate official confirmation specific to SA. The app therefore
**does not repeat a fixed daily schedule or assume a 12-hour recurrence**.
The observed in-game countdown is the prediction's reference.

## Abyss bosses

Watcher Kaira, Guardian Lord Nahma and Executors Tamasa/Argo/Kaira use the
Global client records published by
[AION 2 Gaming Tools — Abyss](https://aion2.gaming.tools/maps/20).
They are labeled **Global · SA reference**, with times interpreted on the
regional clock. These spawns have not been individually confirmed on SA.
They are not live spawn or kill detection.

## Existing agendas

Only a recognized original agenda receives these additions automatically.
Disabled-event choices are retained and the previous agenda is backed up.
Custom agendas remain intact. To use the new reference with a custom
installation, choose **South America** in Events.
