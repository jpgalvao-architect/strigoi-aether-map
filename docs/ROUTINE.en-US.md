# Routine

Overview lists characters identified by the game and keeps basic and charged
Odyle Energy balances separate. Records stay on this PC, scoped to each
character, server, and region.

## Sync your character

1. Open Azphelumbra before logging in with your character. Automatic reading
   requires Npcap.
2. Log in and open **Routine** from the sidebar.
3. Wait for character identification and the initial balance. If the app
   started after your character logged in, return to character selection
   and log in again.

The balance can arrive before the character's name. Routine waits for the
next identification before assigning it. Missing information shows
**Waiting for a reading**; a zero received from the game shows as zero.

**Saved record** shows an earlier reading and its time. Routine does not
estimate recharges while a character is offline.

## Choose your overlay

1. Open **Customize Routine** and enable **Show Routine overlay**, or use
   **Routine** in the floating emblem's quick menu.
2. Choose **Compact Pulse** or **Strip** and the resources to track.
3. In game, use the layout icon to switch styles. The arrow collapses or
   expands the panel; the padlock locks its position.

Drag the title or emblem. Style, resources, lock, and position are saved.
Overlays follow Alt+Tab and recognized-menu hiding. Hiding the overlay does
not stop sync. The overlay follows your in-game character; browsing another
saved record does not change the character being measured.

## Current scope

The initial basic and charged Odyle list matched a real SA login. Absolute
updates and records with one component at zero have controlled tests;
SA consumption validation is tracked separately. Caps, recharge forecasts,
resets, and dungeon, key, or arena counters are not assumed from other regions.

Reading is passive and shares the Combat and Pets collector. It does not
query a public character API, send commands to the game, or record raw
packets. Turn off **Sync automatically with the game** in **Customize
Routine** to disable only this consumer.
