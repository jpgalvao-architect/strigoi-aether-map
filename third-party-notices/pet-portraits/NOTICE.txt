Factual pet IDs, English names, families and monster-source links are referenced
to the client snapshot indexed by https://aion2.app/db/pets (2026-09-18, build 220).
Only factual identities/links are used; site code, guides and descriptions are
not copied. Per-image original URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
data-sources/pet-portraits.json.

This catalogue does not certify South America availability, live presence,
regional costs, drop probability or world/map coordinates. Such evidence is
tracked separately. Unknown information must not be presented as zero.
